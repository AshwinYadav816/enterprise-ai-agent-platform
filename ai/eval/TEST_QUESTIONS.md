# Manual test: real answers from the evaluation documents

The documents in `eval/docs/` describe a fictional company, Acme Corporation, generated by
`make_docs.py`. The numbers are deliberately unusual: a correct answer can only come from the
documents. The same questions are in `dataset.jsonl` for the automated run (`README.md`).

## Setup

1. Backend with `AI_MODE=real`, Qdrant running.
2. Sign in as **admin@acme-demo.com**, open **Documents**, upload the 7 files from `eval/docs/`
   with visibility **Employee** (everyone can see them). Wait until each one is **Ready**.

## 1. Answers that must come from the documents (check the answer AND the citation)

| ask | expected answer | cited file |
|---|---|---|
| How many days of paid annual leave do employees get? | 24 days | hr_policy.pdf p.1 |
| How many unused leave days can I carry over, and until when? | 6 days, by 31 March | hr_policy.pdf p.1 |
| Which days must hybrid employees be in the office? | Monday and Wednesday | hr_policy.pdf p.3 |
| What is the notice period for managers after probation? | 60 days | hr_policy.pdf p.4 |
| What is the hotel limit in Tokyo? | 210 USD per night | travel_policy.docx |
| Can I fly business class on a long flight? | No; premium economy for 6 h or more | travel_policy.docx |
| How must containers run? | as a non-root user | it_security_policy.pdf p.3 |
| How quickly must I report a lost laptop? | within 1 hour, to security@acme-demo.com | it_security_policy.pdf p.3 |
| What is the Project Atlas budget and how much has been spent? | 380,000 USD, 61% (231,800 USD) | project_atlas_status.docx |
| Which region had the highest Q3 2026 revenue? | East, 143,200 USD | q3_sales.xlsx |
| How much is the referral bonus and when is it paid? | 1,500 USD, after 90 days | benefits_overview.pdf p.2 |
| Where do new employees collect their laptop? | IT desk, room 3B | onboarding_guide.pptx slide 2 |

Click a `[1]` chip: the popover must show the right file and page, and the passage must
contain the answer.

**Follow-up:** ask "How many days of annual leave do I get?" and then "And how many can I carry
over?". The second answer must understand "how many" refers to leave (6 days).

## 2. Questions it must REFUSE ("I couldn't find this in your documents")

| ask | why it's a trap |
|---|---|
| What is the company's parental leave policy? | HR policy covers other leave, not parental |
| How many vacation days do interns get? | only full-time / part-time are covered |
| Does the health insurance cover dental treatment? | insurance is mentioned, dental is not |
| Who is the CEO of Acme? | only the COO is named |
| What was Acme's Q4 2026 revenue? | only Q2 and Q3 exist |
| Does Acme offer stock options to employees? | not in any document |
| What is the office dress code? | not in any document |
| Who won the football world cup in 2022? | general knowledge: must not answer |

A refusal shows **no Sources list**. If it answers any of these with a number or a name, note it
down: that's exactly what the evaluation run measures and tunes.

## 3. Tools

- **Meetings:** paste `eval/transcripts/atlas_weekly_sync.txt`. Expect:
  - decision: launch stays on 1 December, online payments may come later;
  - action items: Rohit fixes the dashboard query (31 Oct), Rohit requests staging (24 Oct),
    Sara runs the 5,000-user load test, Priya sends the pilot survey (27 Oct) and checks with
    the vendor weekly;
  - open question: dark mode before launch?

  No invented owners or dates.
- **Reports:** Sprint report, period "Sprint 7 (6–17 Oct)", instruction "Project Atlas". Expect
  facts from project_atlas_status.docx with citations (40 pilot customers, 2 open bugs, 61% budget,
  vendor API risk). Then a report on something not in the documents, e.g. "Project Neptune":
  expect a refusal, not an invented report.
- **Email:** "Draft an email to the team about the new hotel limits for travel". Expect 150 USD
  and 210 USD for London, New York and Tokyo, and placeholders like [Name] instead of invented names.
